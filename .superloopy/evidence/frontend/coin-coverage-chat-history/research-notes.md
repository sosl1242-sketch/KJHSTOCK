# Research method and scope

Universe captured from Binance USD-M and COIN-M exchangeInfo on 2026-09-06.
Crypto rows exclude TradFi. Exact contract underlyings and confirmed numeric
denominations are retained; similar tickers are not silently merged.

New entries: A 137, B 140, C 108, D 60, E 12 (457 total). Existing 69 retained.
All introductions are original Korean summaries, <=300 Unicode characters.
Each source appears in coin-profile-sources.json and the catalog next to its text.
Private research notes preserve identity and role findings. C was searched
individually using official project docs and official exchange descriptions.

Special C checks: SONIC vs S, US vs SUI gas, STABLE vs USDT gas, USTC depeg,
STG redemption, SSV current ETH fees versus legacy SSV clusters, SLX/Sport.fun
and other difficult names delegated to E. Four Chinese tickers checked against
official exchange announcements. Unibase GitBook markdown whitepaper fetched
directly after HTML extraction failed. TURBO link explicitly labels community
documentation. SOON staking language corrected to planned after independent review.
PUMPBTC's unavailable Alpha URL was replaced with a same-contract XT announcement;
MARSCOIN was matched to its exact Binance listing instead of the older namesake.

The verification date denotes document review, not a code audit or certification
of project claims. Where primary sources state only an ecosystem/meme role,
descriptions do not invent fees, governance rights, or guaranteed returns.
